Professional Experience

Field Analyst at Inicio

I am currently working as a Field Analyst at Inicio, a French startup specializing in project origination, particularly in renewable energy. I started working with them as a freelancer in September 2024 and transitioned to a full-time position on January 1st, 2025.

Inicio leverages an algorithm to identify potential land sites, and my role is to de-risk these sites before passing them on to the sales team who meet with clients and connect them with developers. I focus on the Italian sector, and my work is divided into two main tasks:

  • Land research and analysis based on algorithm results
  • Development of analysis methodologies and new solutions to improve quality and productivity

Earlier this year, I participated in the due diligence process when Inicio raised €4 million in funding. The startup environment has been incredible for discovering new techniques and tools, constantly expanding my skills and capabilities.

London Workshop 2024

In April 2024, I participated in a workshop in London as part of the Master's program in Geomatics at Cergy Pontoise. The workshop took place at Royal Holloway University in Egham and focused on flood risk due to submersion in the London area and its surroundings. During the week-long workshop, we worked on creating a scenario with responses for the short, medium, and long term.

This experience helped me develop my English language skills and gave me insight into how other countries approach risk management and emergency response strategies.
